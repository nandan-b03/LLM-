A Hybrid Dual-Intelligence Framework: Coupling Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN) with Context-Aware Large Language Models (LLM) for Explainable and Multilingual Agro-Advisory

Integration Architecture, Formal Methodology, and Algorithmic Guardrails for Plant Pathology

1. Research Gaps & Novel Contributions

Conventional automated plant pathology frameworks rely predominantly on deep Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) trained to classify leaf imagery into discrete categorical labels. While effective at high-dimensional visual pattern recognition, traditional implementations present significant limitations when deployed in practical agricultural environments:

  • The "Black-Box" Diagnostic Deficit: CNNs yield raw classification indices or static text snippets lacking clinical explanation, pathophysiological reasoning, or nuanced severity assessments.
  • Absence of Conversational Context: Farmers cannot interrogate the diagnosis, request customized irrigation schedules, or explore preventative alternatives for neighboring healthy flora.
  • Uncalibrated Epistemic Uncertainty: Traditional vision classifiers output predictions without communicating classification uncertainty to end-users, risking severe economic loss when low-confidence inferences are acted upon uncritically.
  • Linguistic and Literacy Barriers: Diagnostic systems predominantly operate in English, excluding non-English-literate farmers across key agricultural demographics.
Key Innovation: To resolve these gaps, this work proposes a Hybrid Dual-Intelligence Architecture. The vision subsystem (5-stage Convolutional Deep Neural Network) handles objective, high-speed visual feature extraction and label prediction, while an integrated conversational Large Language Model (LLM) layer acts as a context-aware clinical agro-advisory agent providing structured 7-point clinical explanations, multi-turn follow-up dialogue, multilingual translation (English, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu), and voice interaction.

3. Mathematical and Algorithmic Formulation

The dual-intelligence pipeline is formally defined across three coupled mathematical stages:

Stage I: Visual Classification & Epistemic Probability Extraction

Given an input leaf image matrix $I \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W \times C}$ (preprocessed to $50 \times 50 \times 3$), the CNN parameterized by weights $\mathbf{W}$ maps $I$ through sequential convolution, spatial pooling, and fully-connected dense transformations:

\mathbf{z} = f_{\mathbf{W}}(I) \in \mathbb{R}^{K}, \quad \quad P(y = k \mid I) = \frac{e^{z_k}}{\sum_{j=1}^K e^{z_j}}

The primary prediction $\hat{y}$ and associated epistemic confidence $c$ are determined by:

\hat{y} = \arg\max_{k \in \mathcal{C}} P(y = k \mid I), \quad \quad c = \max_{k \in \mathcal{C}} P(y = k \mid I)

Stage II: Context-State Vector Construction

The diagnostic state vector $\mathcal{S}$ serializes the visual inference, confidence calibration metric, crop taxonomy, and user-specified regional language code $L \in \{\text{en}, \text{kn}, \text{ta}, \text{te}\}$:

\mathcal{S} = \langle \hat{y}, \, c, \, \text{Crop}(\hat{y}), \, \text{SeverityFlag}(c) \rangle

where the confidence thresholding function $\text{SeverityFlag}(c)$ injects algorithmic uncertainty constraints:

\text{SeverityFlag}(c) = \begin{cases} \text{CONFIRMED\_ADVISORY}, & c \ge 0.60 \\ \text{LOW\_CONFIDENCE\_VERIFICATION\_REQUIRED}, & c < 0.60 \end{cases}

Four specialized, decoupled modules were engineered and integrated into the Flask microservice framework without mutating or retraining the underlying CNN:

5. Algorithmic Guardrails & Safety Policies

To prevent hazardous hallucination and chemical mismanagement in agronomic environments, the following safety constraints are systematically enforced:

  1. Bounded Medical/Chemical Disclaimers: The system prompt explicitly prohibits prescribing absolute pesticide or fungicide dosages without mandating local agricultural extension officer verification.
  2. Chemical Admixture Restrictions: Prohibits unverified advice regarding mixing synthetic chemical compounds.
  3. Confidence-Calibrated Verification: Predictions exhibiting $c < 0.60$ render with high-visibility cautionary UI banners stating that physical clinical inspection is essential.
  4. High-Availability Deterministic Fallbacks: In the event of API throttling or offline field conditions, the system defaults to verified agronomic non-chemical cultural practices (e.g., pruning, sub-canopy drip irrigation, rotational planting).
